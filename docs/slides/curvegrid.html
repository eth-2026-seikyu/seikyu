A receivable that lives
and dies on-chain.

Seikyu is a marketplace for unpaid invoices.

A business gets paid today; an investor buys the debt at a discount and collects in full on the due date.

One ERC-721 per invoice — minted at listing, transferred once, burned at settlement.

The problem

The work is done.
The money is 60 days away.

  • A supplier delivers today and is paid in 60 to 90 days
  • That receivable is a real asset, and it sits in a spreadsheet
  • A spreadsheet row can be sold twice, and the buyer cannot audit it

The solution

One token per invoice, for the whole life of the debt

  • Minted when the supplier lists it · transferred exactly once, to an investor the contract has checked · burned the moment the debtor settles

How it works

The life of one receivable

The asset

The listing lives on-chain, not in a database

  • One ERC-721 minted when the supplier lists it
  • Self-held by the market contract: _mint(address(this), id)
  • Burned the instant the debtor settles

The controls

Programmable rules live in the transfer itself

  • Every transfer runs through ERC-721's _update hook
  • Rejects investors who haven't passed World ID
  • Caps each person at 3 open invoices; mint and burn are exempt

No escrow, ever

950 now, instead of 1,000 later

  • buy() sends the price straight to the supplier
  • settle() pays the current owner in full
  • One tx pays, burns the token, retires the ENS name

The states

Six states — four on-chain, two derived

  • Listed, Funded, Paid, Cancelled are written on-chain
  • Overdue and Expired-unsold are derived from the due date
  • Marking overdue extends the ENS name 30 days

  • All three contracts are Sourcify exact-match verified
  • The full issue-to-settle lifecycle ran live on Sepolia for the demo
